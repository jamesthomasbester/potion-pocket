# Potion Pocket / provisional art direction
Working identity: **Little Lux — a pocket potion shop**. Contemporary neighbourhood apothecary, warm ivory architecture, evergreen joinery, apricot accents, lilac moonflowers.

Palette: ink #243e46, cream #fff4dc, deep teal #285958, mint #72c9a0, peach #f6ac78, lavender #b5a0ed, soil #9c6753, gold #f2cd70. Two-tone shading, soft oval contact shadows, 2–3 logical-pixel outlines, no texture noise. Light from upper left.

Projection: fixed 2:1 dimetric floor, basis (48,24), (-48,24). Portrait 540×960 design canvas. Furniture bottom-centre pivots; nominal floor tile 96×48. Characters 30×56, heads about 40% of height, expressive dot eyes, rounded apron bodies and sneakers. Three role hats/accessories: leaf gardener, lilac brewer, gold cashier. Customer colour variants share geometry.

Kit: procedural layered CanvasItem drawing with transparent surroundings, independently reusable floor diamond, wall, shelf, bottle (round energy / diamond dream), herb (paired leaves / star flower), plot, cauldron, counter, person. No flattened scene background. Future raster exports: RGBA, 2× design scale, no baked ground shadow, bottom-centre contact anchor; shared reference is the actual initial engine screenshot. Existing content is not used.

UI: native Godot controls and bundled default font; headings 26–32, body 18–20, secondary minimum 16 logical pixels. 12/16/24 spacing grid. Principal buttons at least 56 logical pixels tall, lower screen. Dark teal enabled, muted sage disabled, peach selected; explicit prices and benefits. Shape and labels supplement colour. Safe area inset applied on Android.

Animation: time-based squash/bob, cauldron bubbles, growth stages, carried ingredient/bottle journeys and strolling customers. Shared geometry ensures frame alignment and avoids generated-frame flicker. No sprite-frame animation generator used. Feedback sounds are original synthesized PCM WAVs, generated by tools/make_audio.py and imported by Godot; low-volume chord ambience, mute persisted. Haptics only Android, short transaction pulse.

Review: art remains provisional until user feedback. No speculative generated batches. Main risks to inspect: room readability, control density, character scale and empty vertical space on tall phones.

Touch revision after desktop review: principal controls are 64 logical pixels high (about 49 dp on the connected Pixel 6); Sound and development reset remain secondary 48-pixel controls. The artwork area yields height first on 9:16 screens.

Final kit review: hiring adds a separate hat-coded helper; the proprietor remains visible. Renovation changes the sign to gold, replaces the floor and refreshes walls. A visible vial fills with brew progress. Customers walk in from and out toward the right edge. Character paths are illustrative, not collision simulation.
